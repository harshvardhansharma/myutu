Myutu — face reference

Hand-authored SVG translation of face_lab/face_lab.ino → shapes(). All ten expressions, resting state. This file is the design source of truth.

How to use this file

  1. Edit the SVG here first. Every shape carries a comment naming the exact C++ call it came from.
  2. Port the change into face_lab/face_lab.ino → shapes(), keeping the primitive the comment names.
  3. Then port the same change into myutu_pet/myutu_pet.ino, which carries its own copy of the drawing code.

Every face is drawn at rest: fOpen = 1.0 (eyes fully open), fSacX = fSacY = 0 (gaze centred), bob = 0, per-face oscillations at t = 0. Blink and saccade apply to every face and are not shown.

This is a reference sheet — nothing here animates. Motion is described in words under each card.

The panel has no alpha blending, so the C++ builds its bloom by drawing the whole face three times — grow 5 in HALO2, grow 2 in HALO1, grow 0 in CORE. The SVG fakes the same look with one <filter>; see #glow in the shared defs.

neutral case NEUTRAL

idle

Still apart from the global blink and saccade, plus the 1.5 s vertical bob shared by every face.

surprised case SURPRISED

one tap

A held breath: the eye radius pulses ±1.5 px on a 420 ms sine. Brows only appear while fOpen > 0.35, so they vanish mid-blink.

happy case HAPPY

two quick taps

A pleased wiggle: wig = 2·sin(t/380) rocks the two brows in counterphase and slides the mouth ±2 px with the left brow.

dizzy case DIZZY

shake

Both X's orbit on a 170 ms cycle — left by (5·sin, 4·cos), right by the negative, so they counter-rotate. The mouth wave scrolls, its phase driven straight off t.

sleepy case SLEEPY

idle 20 s

Losing the fight: droop swings 0.62 → 1.00 on a 2.4 s sine, so the lids sag and the eye catches itself again. Drawn at droop = 0.81.

sleeping case SLEEPING

idle 45 s

Breathes on a 1.4 s sine: lids and mouth ride ±4 px and the mouth radius swells 4 → 6. Three Z's loop on a 2.6 s cycle, each rising 50 px, drifting 20 px right and growing from text size 1 to 3.

curious case CURIOUS

tilt and hold ~0.6 s

Actively searching: on a 1.5 s sine the gaze sweeps look = ±12 px and the head tilt rocks ±8 px, pushing the eyes in opposite directions. Drawn at the centre of the sweep, where the only asymmetry left is the smaller right eye.

scared case SCARED

free fall

Trembles: the two eyes shift ±2 px in opposition on a fast 120 ms sine, so they pinch together and apart. The mouth wave scrolls at 1.5× the dizzy rate.

love case LOVE

three quick taps

Two hearts drift up the right side on a 2.2 s loop, half a cycle apart: y falls 88 → 40, size grows 6 → 12, and x weaves ±10 px through one full sine as it climbs.

angry case ANGRY

shake again within 4 s

Both sparks pulse their radius ±2 px on a fast 130 ms sine — the small one always 2 px behind the large one.

Translated from face_lab/face_lab.ino at commit time. Known approximations: thickLine is a stroked <line> with round caps, where the C++ smears a 1 px line across ±t/2 in both x and y, giving square, slightly over-long ends; the glow is a Gaussian blur rather than three concentric redraws; and the sleeping Z's use a system monospace font in place of the Adafruit GFX 5×7 bitmap. Everything else is exact integer arithmetic lifted from shapes(), including its truncating (int) casts and integer divisions.
